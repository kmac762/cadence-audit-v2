What changed

The interface now follows the established audits.cadenceseo.com visual language: white Cadence masthead, brand-spectrum rule, dark field canvas, serif display headings, orbit/signal motif, and dark outlined audit cards.

The Content Freshness module is now always visible in reports. In site-sample mode it also gets a small dedicated article sample (up to eight sitemap-discovered article URLs) so the module does not disappear merely because the balanced technical sample missed the blog. A zero-article result is shown as a coverage limitation rather than hidden.
